HOW TO PLAY

Everyone gets 7 cards. On your turn play one card that matches the top card by color, number or symbol, or play a Wild. Can’t play? Tap the deck to draw one – if it fits you may play it, otherwise your turn passes. Skip skips the next player, Reverse flips direction (with 2 players it acts like Skip), Draw Two makes the next player draw 2 and lose their turn. Wild Draw Four lets you pick the color and the next player draws 4 – only allowed when you have no card of the current color. When you’re down to one card tap ONE! – if someone catches you first, you draw 2. First to empty their hand wins the round. In First to 500 the winner scores the cards left in everyone’s hands (numbers face value, action cards 20, wilds 50).
